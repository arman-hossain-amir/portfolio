Practice

From construction drawings to permit sets in New York.

I trained as an architect at North South University in Dhaka and spent six years in practice there, moving from producing construction documents to leading projects and running site supervision. Most of that work was residential, interior and hospitality — the kind of project where the drawings have to survive contact with a real contractor on a real site.

I moved to the United States to study project management at Northeastern University, learning the scheduling, budgeting and resource-planning side of the same work. At Quantum Design & Safety in New York I designed three accessory dwelling units for the city's pre-approved plan library, produced the full interior drawing set for a single-family house upstate, and coordinated site-specific safety plans and egress drawings for city construction projects — back in front of code, drawings and inspectors, this time under the New York City Department of Buildings rather than Rajuk.

Alongside the design work I ran the firm's site safety school, a DOB-approved SST course provider — scheduling classes, issuing worker and supervisor cards, and growing monthly revenue by about two thirds by filling the calendar and promoting the courses.

I work in AutoCAD, Revit, SketchUp and Rhino, render in Lumion, and schedule in Microsoft Project. I speak English and Bengali fluently and conversational Hindi.
