Kazipara Apartment

Kazipara, Mirpur, Dhaka — interior design for the living and dining rooms of an apartment

Interior design for the living and dining rooms of an apartment in Kazipara, Mirpur, in dark-stained timber, white walls and soft brown upholstery.

The living room is framed by a faceted timber feature wall with a ring mirror and lit by a linear geometric chandelier; a hanging egg chair sits by the window, opposite the television wall. A gridded timber and glass partition separates it from the dining room, where an oval table runs between tall timber doors, geometric wall art and a pair of large woven disc floor lamps.

Location
Kazipara, Mirpur, Dhaka
Role
