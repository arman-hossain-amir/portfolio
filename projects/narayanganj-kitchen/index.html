Narayanganj Kitchen

College Road, Narayanganj — a kitchen remodel for an apartment

A kitchen remodel for an apartment on College Road, Narayanganj. New cream lacquer cabinets and open shelving, black stone worktops and a patterned tile backsplash were fitted to the existing room, with a tall pantry and appliance unit and under-cabinet lighting along the worktop.

The renders are sectional views through the room, one for each wall, used to check the cabinet runs, the appliances and the door and window positions together.

Project architect: Arman H. Amir, ZED Architecture & Construction.
