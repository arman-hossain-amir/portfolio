Savar Duplex

Savar, Dhaka — a duplex house on a narrow plot, behind a street front of stone and timber louvres

A house on a narrow plot in Savar, Dhaka. The street front is a stone-clad frame filled with vertical timber louvres and recessed balconies, set against a plain white flank wall, with planting along the roof terrace.

At ground level the car parks in a tall hall beside an open timber stair, with a planted water feature at its foot. Further in, a narrow pool runs beneath the stair toward a small planted court. The cutaway shows one bedroom suite: bedroom, walk-in wardrobe and bathroom in one compact plan.
