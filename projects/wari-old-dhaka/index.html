Wari Rooftop and Lobby

Wari, Old Dhaka — a rooftop terrace and entrance lobby for a residential building

A rooftop terrace and a ground-floor reception for a residential building in Wari, Old Dhaka — from the entrance the residents use every day to the roof they gather on in the evening.

The roof is laid out for use after dark. A slatted white pergola hung with trailing plants covers the dining tables and barbecue, planters line the parapet, a mesh enclosure screens the service corner, and an oval path of inset floor lights loops the open deck between the stair and water-tank houses.

Downstairs, the entrance is clad in large-format beige stone with black marble accents, with a band of rough-cut stone above the glazed reception office and a diamond-patterned feature wall at the lift lobby.
