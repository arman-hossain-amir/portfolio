Noubahini School Library

Bangladesh Noubahini School and College, Chattogram — a library for the school, planned as four connected rooms

A library design for Bangladesh Noubahini School and College in Chattogram, planned as four rooms within one rectangular floor so that quiet reading, group work and presentations each have their own space.

One room is given to long reading tables. Another holds a green, branching shelf-and-bench island with hexagonal stools for younger readers. A third contains the issue desk and stacks, and the fourth is a projection area with curved built-in seating around a round table. Timber shelving lines the perimeter walls throughout.
