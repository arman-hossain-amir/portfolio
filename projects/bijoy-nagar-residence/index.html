Bijoy Nagar Residence

Bijoy Nagar, Dhaka — interior design for an apartment

Interior design for an apartment in Bijoy Nagar, central Dhaka, in grey, white and dark-stained timber.

Open black shelving frames the media wall and separates the living room from the dining area, which is seen through a gridded timber and glass screen. One joinery run along the dining wall holds the refrigerator, oven, washing machine and open storage beside a small breakfast table. In the bedroom a stone-clad panel carries the television, and a lit dressing niche is built in between the wardrobes.

Project architect: Arman H. Amir. Designed at Cornerstone Design Studio, which holds the rights to the drawings.
