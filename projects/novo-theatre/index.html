Novo Theatre

Bangabandhu Sheikh Mujibur Rahman Novo Theatre, Barishal — a 6.2-acre public science centre for the Ministry of Science & Technology. Thesis project, B.Arch final year

A public science centre organised around a planetarium dome and a long exhibition bar. The masterplan splits the riverside site into a public arrival court, an exhibition and learning zone, and a quieter research and administration wing, so school groups and researchers can use the building at the same time without crossing paths.

Programme covers the planetarium, exhibition gallery, lecture hall, library, research lab, cafeteria, gift shop and administration.

Thesis project, B.Arch, North South University.
