P-11Interiors

Khulna Residence

Khulna, Bangladesh — interior design for a family home

Interior design for a family home in Khulna, in a light palette of white lacquer, pale marble and dark timber.

The living room is organised around an arched alcove lined with botanical wallpaper, with a diagonal timber bookcase and a sculptural white lounge chair; the sitting area opposite is framed by a bevelled mirror wall in dark timber and brass. A long marble dining table runs beside a glazed display kitchen, and a family lounge finishes the plan with a gallery wall and a slatted timber media unit.

Project architect: Arman H. Amir, ZED Architecture & Construction.
