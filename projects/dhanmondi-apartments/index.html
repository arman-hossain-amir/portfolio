Two Apartments, Dhanmondi

Dhanmondi, Dhaka — living-room interiors for two apartments, the first a joint venture with Boobun – Architecture & Construction

Two apartment interiors in Dhanmondi, Dhaka.

The first, a joint venture with Boobun – Architecture & Construction, is a living room in terracotta upholstery against plaster-white panelled walls, with large bird and leaf murals, a marble media wall and timber-framed folding glass doors that open the room to the dining area. A cluster of globe pendants hangs from a recessed, mirrored ceiling.

The second is quieter: grey and white throughout, with moulded wall panels, a mirrored wall that doubles the room, a sculpted timber media console and a sheer-curtained window wall.
