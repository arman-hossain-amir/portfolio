LCLS Lawyer's Chamber

Gulshan-1, Dhaka — reception and waiting area for a law chamber, a joint venture with Dreero.aec

Reception and waiting area for a law chamber in Gulshan-1. A barrel-vaulted ceiling with coffered ribs and cove lighting gives the long, narrow room a sense of order and draws the eye to a timber-framed window wall at its end.

The reception desk is a curved dark-timber counter with brass inlay and a black marble return, set against white walls and dark doors. Dark timber flooring, a low lounge sofa and framed landscape prints keep the waiting side calm.

Joint venture between ZED Architecture & Construction and Dreero.aec – Architecture, Engineering & Construction. Project architects: Arman H. Amir (IAB AA-681) and Tanmay Hassan (IAB AH-370).
