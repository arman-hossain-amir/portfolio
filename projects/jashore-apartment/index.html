P-09Interiors

Jashore Apartment

Jashore, Bangladesh — interior design for a family apartment: living and dining rooms, a study-bedroom and a second bedroom

Interior design for a family apartment in Jashore, shown here in two parts: the living and dining spaces, and a bedroom suite.

The shared rooms are held together by white wall panelling, walnut slatted ceilings and thin gold trim. A marble and timber media wall anchors the living room; the dining table sits under a recessed ceiling with a square geometric pendant, screened from the entrance by a mirrored cabinet partition. In the study-bedroom, full-height lacquered wardrobes run alongside an open bookshelf and desk.

The second bedroom pairs a warm stone feature wall with white shaker wardrobes, a built-in entry bench with coat hooks, a dressing table with an arched mirror, and a hanging egg chair by the window.

Project architect: Arman H. Amir, ZED Architecture & Construction.
