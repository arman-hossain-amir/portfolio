ADU Models X, Y and Z

New York City — three detached accessory dwelling units for the city's pre-approved plan library, 2025

Three detached accessory dwelling units designed for New York City's pre-approved plan library — the programme that lets a homeowner build a backyard unit from a vetted design instead of commissioning a scheme from scratch. Each had to sit on an ordinary rear yard, clear zoning and energy code, and be buildable as a prefabricated lumber-framed unit.

Model X is a 643 sq ft one-bedroom on a 16 by 40 ft rectangular footprint under a single-pitch roof. Model Y turns the plan into an L, adding a walk-in closet and a dedicated office that converts to a second bedroom. Model Z stacks 776 sq ft over two floors, with a double-height living space and a bedroom and bathroom on each level, so two households can share it without sharing a bathroom.

All three run on a high-efficiency heat pump, carry insulation above NYC Energy Conservation Code minimums, and use glazing at U-factor 0.27 or lower and SHGC 0.4 or lower — compatible with Passive House and ENERGY STAR. Each plan sets aside a location for a future ADA-compliant ramp and specifies 60-minute fire-rated interior panels.
