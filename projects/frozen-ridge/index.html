P-02Interiors

365 Frozen Ridge Road

Newburgh, New York — interior design and documentation for a proposed single-family house, 2026

A proposed single-family house on a 0.945-acre lot in Orange County. The architectural plans and elevations came from another team; I took the approved shell and designed the interiors — layouts, finishes, joinery and lighting — then produced the full interior design drawing set and the renders used to get the client to sign off.

The plan turns on a double-height living space with a plaster fireplace column at its centre, open to the kitchen and dining on one side and to the stair on the other. Thirty sheets in all: furniture and lighting layouts, HVAC and curtain-rail layouts, floor and wall tile setting-out, and elevation details for the fireplace wall, the kitchen, bedroom joinery, four bathrooms and the laundry. I also prepared the material and bill-of-quantities schedule that priced the package.

Architectural plans and elevations by others. Interior design, ID drawing set, material schedule and renders by me.
