Uttara Residential Building

Uttara, Dhaka — a residential building on a narrow city plot, one three-bedroom apartment per floor

A residential building on a narrow plot in Uttara, Dhaka. The street elevation is composed as interlocking frames: a white frame of stacked balconies on one side, a dark stone-clad frame with recessed, planted terraces on the other, and an open roof pavilion with a circular cut-out above.

The typical floor is a single three-bedroom apartment arranged around a central living and dining space, with the stair, lift and lobby grouped in one corner of the plan.

Project architect: Arman H. Amir. Designed at Cornerstone Design Studio, which holds the rights to the drawings.
