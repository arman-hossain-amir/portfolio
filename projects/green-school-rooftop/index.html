Green School Rooftops

Mohammadpur Green School, Dhaka — two rooftops turned into outdoor play spaces for the children

Two rooftop designs for Mohammadpur Green School in Dhaka, turning the flat roofs of the school into outdoor play spaces for the children — two distinct layouts with one aim.

The larger roof, about 1,526 sq ft, is a rectangle around the stair house: a green play surface edged with a blue safety border, swings, a slide, climbing frames and a round play pit, with a long planted canopy for shade. The smaller roof, about 1,026 sq ft, follows an irregular edge beside a pitched-roof section of the building, with see-saws, swings and a shaded strip along its long side. Both are enclosed by a tall mesh safety fence above the parapet.

Project architect: Arman H. Amir. Designed at Cornerstone Design Studio, which holds the rights to the drawings.
