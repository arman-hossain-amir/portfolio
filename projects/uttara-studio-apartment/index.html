P-07Interiors

Uttara Apartment Interior

Uttara, Dhaka — full fit-out of the lower floor of a duplex, in a warm beige palette. Completed 2023

A full interior fit-out of the lower floor of a duplex in Uttara, Dhaka, for Lithee Group. The palette of warm beige, pale marble, dark timber and brass runs through every room.

The living room centres on a marble and timber television wall. Beside the stair opening, with its black curved railing, a family lounge sits under swagged curtains and a white coffered ceiling. The master bedroom has a glazed, gridded wardrobe wall under a round cove ceiling and chandelier, and opens to a dressing area and a bathroom with a freestanding tub, a rain shower and a laundry corner.

Drawings covered furniture layouts, ceiling and lighting plans, and joinery details for each room.

Location
Uttara, Dhaka
