P-05Academic

Z Commercial Complex

Gulshan-1, Dhaka — a 17-storey mixed-use tower on a tight corner plot. Academic project, fifth year

The lower floors are cut back and opened up to give the street a covered public forecourt, with an exhibition gallery and restaurant at the base, rentable office floors above, and a gymnasium in the podium.

The drawing set includes site and floor plans from basement to roof, two full building sections, and external wall sections at roof, typical floor and basement.

Academic project, B.Arch fifth year, North South University.
