P-05Academic

Z Commercial Complex

Gulshan-1, Dhaka — a 17-storey mixed-use tower on a tight corner plot. Academic project, fifth year

The lower floors are cut back and opened up to give the street a covered public forecourt and drop-off. Above it, an exhibition gallery wraps a top-lit atrium that looks down onto the forecourt, with a restaurant at the base, rentable office floors above and a gymnasium in the podium.

Upper floors step and curve at their edges, with planted terraces cut into the facade. The drawing set includes the site and ground-floor plans, floor plans from basement to roof, elevations, two full building sections, and external wall sections at roof, typical floor and basement.
