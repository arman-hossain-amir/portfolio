P-09Interiors

Gulshan-1 Apartment

Gulshan-1, Dhaka — interior design for an apartment: living and dining rooms, kitchen and bedrooms

Interior design for an apartment in Gulshan-1, Dhaka, in white, warm grey and brown leather.

The living room is built around a large circular moon panel set beside a marble television wall with open white shelving, facing a pair of tufted leather sofas. The dining table sits between the living room and an open kitchen wall that holds the refrigerator, washing machine and glazed crockery cabinets, with linear art panels on the walls around it.

In the bedrooms, full-height lacquered wardrobes, a walnut chevron headboard wall, dressing tables with oval mirrors and a desk corner under moon-and-star wall pieces give each room its own character within the one palette.
