P-04Masterplan

Green Farm Resort

Garpara, Manikganj, Bangladesh — a 6.8-acre resort masterplan built around an existing lake. In construction

The plan keeps the lake as the centre of the scheme and arranges the hotel, a three-storey restaurant and conference hall, twenty rentable cottages, a floating bar, an amphitheatre and a children's playground around its edge, linked by a raised timber walkway.

I led the design and coordinated drawings through construction, including the owner's three-storey villa on the same site.

Location
Manikganj, Bangladesh
Role
Lead designer
Site area
6.8 acres
Type

Project architect: Arman H. Amir. Designed at Cornerstone Design Studio, which holds the rights to the drawings.
