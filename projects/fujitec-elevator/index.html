Elevator Cabin Designs

Cabin interior concepts for FUJITEC, Japan — three finish options for an elevator cab

Cabin interior concepts prepared for FUJITEC, the Japanese elevator manufacturer. Three finish schemes — brushed stainless steel, a warm timber tone with copper trim, and black with gold accents — are each shown as flat wall elevations and in perspective, with a matching ceiling light panel and floor finish.
