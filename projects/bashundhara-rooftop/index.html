Bashundhara Rooftop and Entrance

Bashundhara R/A, Dhaka — a rooftop terrace, ground-floor reception and front-yard garden for an existing residential building

A refurbishment of an existing residential building in Bashundhara R/A, in three parts: the bare rooftop turned into a terrace for the residents, a new reception at the foot of the stair, and a planted front yard that gives the building an address on the street.

On the roof, a timber deck under a shade-sail pergola holds the dining tables and barbecue. A flowering trellis wall dresses the stair house, a steel spiral stair climbs to the upper roof, and a dome skylight sits in a checkerboard of grey and white paving. The lighting was planned for evening use as much as for the day.

At street level the old gates and forecourt were reworked into a raised planter with a lit stone name plinth, and a glass reception box at the base of the stair. The reception and front yard have been built; the last three images are photographs of the finished work.

Project architect: Arman H. Amir, ZED Architecture & Construction.
